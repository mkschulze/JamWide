Drop excess frames if the camera produces faster than 10 fps; pad-by-duplicate-last if it produces slower (10 fps is JamTaba's CAMERA_FPS — RESEARCH §1).

4. **Per-frame encode:** Read pixels via `juce::Image::BitmapData` (ARGB on macOS), convert to YUV420P. Drain with `avcodec_receive_packet` in a loop; append packet `data[0..size-1]` to a `std::vector<uint8_t> bitstream`.

5. **Flush encoder:** `avcodec_send_frame(ctx, nullptr)` then drain remaining packets into `bitstream`. This is the raw H.264 NAL bytestream — same as JamTaba's wire format (no AVFormat container, RESEARCH §1).

Iterate the bitstream by parsing NALUs (`av_parser_init(AV_CODEC_ID_H264)` + `av_parser_parse2` is the standard pattern); feed each parsed packet to `avcodec_send_packet` and drain `avcodec_receive_frame` into `AVFrame*` objects.
