<objective>
Fix the receive-path dispatch wire-compatibility bug at src/core/njclient.cpp:2148, where any non-zero/non-FLAC fourcc currently routes silently into start_decode (Vorbis). After this plan, non-OGGv/non-FLAC fourcc streams with a registered RawData_Callback dispatch the 3-event callback sequence (begin/data/end) instead; with no callback registered they are logged and discarded (no Vorbis routing). The existing OGGv + FLAC branch at line 2148 remains in place AFTER the two new branches — this is an INSERTION, not a REPLACEMENT (Landmine L4). Also adds the corresponding WRITE-handler loop at line ~2220 that walks m_rawdata_downloads BEFORE the existing m_downloads loop and fires eventType=1 (data) / eventType=2 (end) on GUID matches.

Purpose: Close SC-6 (the only behavioral change of the phase) so the v1.3 Native Video milestone has a working receive substrate. NinjamZap's wire-compat depends on this dispatch — without it, JamWide receivers that connect to a Ninjamzap-server-flavored room would silently crash the Vorbis decoder on every video keyframe.

Output: A surgical 2-branch insert at line 2148 + a 1-loop insert at line ~2220; an in-process unit test verifying the dispatch + the OGGv/FLAC routing-untouched invariant; no behavioral change to existing audio receivers.
</objective>
