Purpose: This is the testable, self-contained foundation. No grid container, no editor wiring, no popouts, no state persistence in this plan — just the tile-shaped consumers of the distributor subscriptions and the layout math the container will eventually call. The MEMBER-ORDER CONTRACT, proven via Phase 19's CameraPreviewTile, is the single largest UAF risk in the phase; getting it into a separate plan with its own static_assert test prevents Plan 22-02's grid container from accidentally reordering members during the larger integration.
